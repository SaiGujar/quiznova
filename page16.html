Detailed Solutions

Q1: What is the powerhouse of the cell?

Correct Answer: Mitochondria

Explanation: Mitochondria generate most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions.

Q2: Which of the following is a scalar quantity?

Correct Answer: Speed

Explanation: Speed has magnitude only, whereas velocity, force, and acceleration have both magnitude and direction.

Q3: What is the chemical symbol for Gold?

Correct Answer: Au

Explanation: Au comes from the Latin word "aurum", meaning "shining dawn".

Q4: What is the value of Pi (π) approximately?

Correct Answer: 3.14159

Explanation: Pi represents the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.

Q5: Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?

Correct Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere.
